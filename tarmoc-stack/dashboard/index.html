Reports

Daily health, weekly resource usage, and monthly availability reports export to PDF, CSV, or Excel. Configure a schedule to have them delivered automatically.

Activity logs

Every restart, threshold change, and server action is recorded here with the user, IP, and result — required for every dangerous action.
